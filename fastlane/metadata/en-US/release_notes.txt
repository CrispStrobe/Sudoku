Thermo Sudoku — a new variant.

Digits climb from each thermometer's bulb to its tip. Every size up to 9×9, and most boards need no starting digits at all: the thermometers alone are enough to pin the grid down.

Also in this update:

• A much bigger board on small and folding phones. On a compact screen the grid had been taking about half the width — it now fills it.
• Landscape is a real layout instead of a squeeze: the controls sit beside the board rather than above and below it.
• Everything holds together at large accessibility text sizes, in English and German.
• Explain the solve fits the screen it is on, so the whole board and the explanation are visible at once.
• Pencil marks are readable again on 10×10 and 12×12 boards.
• Killer puzzles now draw their cages correctly in every build.
• Fixed a startup problem that could leave the game showing a blank screen.
